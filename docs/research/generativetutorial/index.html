Generative Tutorial: Towards Live Contextualized Visual Instructions for Physical Tasks

Muzhe Wu*, Zuchen Li*, Xu Wang, Anhong Guo
Preprint 2026

Generative Tutorial explores how live visual instructions can adapt to a person’s workspace and ongoing physical task. Informed by an evaluation of image and video generation across 15 tasks, the augmented-reality prototype generates goal images and demonstration videos using observed workspace context and predicted outcomes of earlier actions. A study with 24 participants found improved task performance quality, stronger perceived correspondence with the workspace, and shorter step-confirmation intervals compared with pre-authored guidance. The findings also examine how contextual resemblance, generation errors, and delivery timing shape users’ interpretation and trust.
